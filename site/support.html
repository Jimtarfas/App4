Getting a good identification

  • Place the coin on a plain, dark surface in soft, even light. Avoid glare.
  • Fill the circle with the coin and hold the phone steady until it's sharp.
  • Photograph both sides. The date and mint mark are often small, so get close.
  • If a result looks wrong, scan again with better light. Low-confidence results are labelled in the app.

About value estimates

Values are AI estimates of a typical retail range for the condition shown, not a professional appraisal or an offer to buy. For coins that may be valuable, have them authenticated and graded by a professional service before buying or selling.

Subscriptions

  • Restore a purchase: open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID you subscribed with.
  • Cancel: open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Coin Values. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.
  • Refunds: refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
