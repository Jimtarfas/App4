Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Coin Values: Coin Identifier ("Coin Values", "the app") identifies coins from photos you take. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. The short version: coin photos are used only to identify the coin, and everything else stays on your iPhone.

Coin photos

When you scan a coin, the app sends the photos of the front and back of the coin to our identification service. The service passes them to Claude, an AI model provided by Anthropic, PBC, which returns the coin's details and an estimated value. The app asks for your permission before your first scan.

  • Photos are used only to identify the coin you scanned.
  • Our identification service does not store your photos. It processes them in real time and keeps only basic, anonymous technical logs (such as how long a request took).
  • Anthropic processes the photos under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to use them to train its models. Anthropic may keep request data for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • We never sell your photos or use them for advertising.

Please photograph only the coin. Avoid including people, documents or other personal information in the frame.

Your collection and scan history

The coins you scan, your collection, your notes and the saved photos are stored only on your iPhone. We have no account system and cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. To confirm that your subscription is active, the app sends the signed transaction record that Apple provides to our identification service with each scan. The service checks it and does not store it.

What we don't do

Coin Values has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking across apps or websites.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only to photograph the coin you want to identify.
  • Photos: if you choose a photo from your library, iOS shows a picker and the app receives only the photo you pick.

You can change camera access at any time in the iOS Settings app, and you can withdraw your consent to AI identification in the app's Settings.

Children

Coin Values is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
