Coin Values: Coin Identifier

Scan old coins and see what they may be worth.

Coin Values is an iPhone app for coin collectors and anyone who has found an old coin in a drawer. Photograph the front and back, and the app identifies the coin and shows:

  • the country, denomination, year and mint mark,
  • the series and metal composition,
  • how rare the coin is, from common to very rare,
  • an estimated value range for the condition shown.

Save the coins you own to your collection and see their total estimated value.

Values are estimates. Coin Values uses AI to estimate a typical retail value range. It is not a professional appraisal; real prices depend on condition, authenticity and demand.

Coin Values Pro

Identifying coins needs a Coin Values Pro subscription, offered weekly or yearly (the yearly plan starts with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers). Prices are shown in the app before you subscribe, and you can cancel anytime in your App Store account settings.
